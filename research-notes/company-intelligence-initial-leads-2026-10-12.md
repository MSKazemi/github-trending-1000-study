## Source handling

In each case the **article is direct primary testimony about what the organization said, shipped, or legally recorded**. No general acceptance rate, causal improvement, real code reuse, carbon savings, full company news coverage, company revenue success or forecast accuracy has been established. The 100 issues each require their *own* official and independently screened retrospective source log, with publication/event dates, de-duplication and absent-month disclosure. This lead set is intentionally **not** the first seven items of the 1,000-news corpus until that corpus independently screens and validates them under [issue #56](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study/issues/56).

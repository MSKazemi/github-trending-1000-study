## Study A and Study B: estimand cards to freeze

### Study A — repository/attention association (NOT causal)
- **Population:** verified distinct repository IDs among a permitted, dated, predeclared Trending capture panel; independent comparison frame analyzed with explicit coverage limits.
- **Predictor:** specified *pre-outcome* baseline repository practice; measure proximity to first Trending exposure.
- **Proposed primary visibility outcome:** signed 30-day difference of log1p(stargazers_count) with a separately reported net star delta; neither is actual reuse.
- **Estimand:** covariate-adjusted association conditional on the observed panel, with uncertainty clustered for repeated records and sensitivity to selection, language, activity and external publicity.
- **Material missing details:** panel access and feasibility, exact first observation rule, controls' probability frame, attrition rules, effect size/power/multiplicity and registered model.

### Study B — controlled task-level reuse-first comparison (if feasible)
- **Population:** prospectively sampled, consented real development tasks **irrespective of whether a reusable solution is known**.
- **Proposed treatment:** assignment to ordinary versus reuse-first among available agent/human workflow conditions.
- **Proposed primary target:** intention-to-treat arm-level ratio **sum independently accepted outcomes / sum nonoverlapping human-active hours**, including failed/abandoned tasks in denominator; report accepted-task probability independently.
- **Safety gates:** frozen independent functional and adverse-case criteria; actual compatible licenses/security/provenance; opt-in data minimization; review burden; follow-up.
- **Material missing details:** task frame, blocking, minimum meaningful effect, pilot precision, accepted-task criteria, statistical model/cluster plan, consent and carbon instrumentation.
- **Interpretation:** independent randomized allocation can support limited task-population causal estimates; public repo observational traces cannot.

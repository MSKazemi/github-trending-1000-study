## Concrete review conclusions

1. **Preserve Draft and issues Open.** Each issue demands a full April–October query and included/excluded-URL trail, independent and contrary sources, publication **and** underlying event date, edition/availability/license boundaries and source/factual review.
2. **Do not merge on green head CI alone.** The repository [merge policy](PR_QUALITY_AND_MERGE_POLICY.md) additionally requires scientific review, no-loss current-main comparison, exact-head and integrated CI; status may change after this snapshot.
3. **Separate vendor E1 from observed E3.** The new ledger entries are source-linked first-party reports with technical qualifications, not verified adoption. Preserve uncertainty and negative/unavailable findings.
4. **Deduplicate corporate ownership/events.** GitHub/Microsoft and Cognition/Windsurf are distinct editorial topics but not unambiguously independent parent-company observations.
5. **Protect the trial denominator.** A useful 2027 agent/IDE/review experiment counts every allocated task, failed patch, refusal and repair effort, not just successful demo PRs. Compare model/compute and license constraints fairly, record human minutes, and only infer lifecycle energy if metered.

## Practical 2027 lessons for other repositories

- Adopt a small, pinned **agent-enabled maintenance task benchmark** (8–12 prespecified fixtures for pilot feasibility, not a statistically powered study), with independent hidden tests and human license/security review.
- Treat agent approval as advisory; block unattended merge into `main` for high-risk changes and require up-to-date CI, human scrutiny and provenance.
- Separate parallel worktrees/IDE integration from independently measured improvements: include reviewer minutes, CI/retries, credential and dependency rights, and follow-up repairs in the cost.
- Keep security boundaries explicit: agent/browser apps must not casually inherit secrets or write credentials; document trusted packages/versions and least-privilege execution.
- Publish negative and null findings honestly and do not translate vendor benchmarks, stars, task volume or token reductions into independently verified reuse or carbon reduction.

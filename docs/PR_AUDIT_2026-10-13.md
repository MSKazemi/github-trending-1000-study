## Practical lesson for this and other repositories

**Green tests are a necessary engineering filter, not a quality certificate.** A strong PR system measures the real cost of integration: independent specification, verified acceptance, supply-chain/legal fit, reviewer effort, preservation of prior work and long-term support. Resist the temptation to optimize for *number of merged PRs*. A paused draft with useful evidence and a clear blocker is preferable to a fast merge that silently corrupts the research record.

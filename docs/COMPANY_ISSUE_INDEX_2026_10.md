## Follow-through and scientific safeguards

- Each company's historical window must be **auditable**; publication and underlying event dates are distinct. Use explicitly recorded gaps when sources or month checks yielded nothing; "nothing found" is not proof of no release.
- Treat vendor claims as announcements, not independently tested results. A documented public roadmap is not a future guarantee. Report alternative hypotheses and forecast falsifiers for 2027–2031.
- Map the company's innovations to independently accepted user tasks, verified downstream reuse, quality and review/maintenance workload, rights/security and full-boundary ecological costs. [GitHub Trending](TRENDING_VALIDITY_AUDIT.md), stars, installs, LLM tokens, fundraising and traffic are **not** sufficient endpoints.
- Reuse [issue #56](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study/issues/56)'s one-item-at-a-time news-evidence workflow; don't duplicate press-release syndications or fabricate citations.

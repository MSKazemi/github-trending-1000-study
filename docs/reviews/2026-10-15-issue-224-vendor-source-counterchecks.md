## Review questions before a scientific close

- Can a qualified reviewer reproduce these dated URLs and identify immutable as-of snapshots, historical job-posting dates and the precise employer/role identity? Unknown dates must remain **unknown**, not backdated.
- Do source pairs use truly independent sources? **No:** job and announcement are employer-origin assertions. Require separate functional task results or a valid independent third party, never count them as two independent confirmations.
- For future prospective data, freeze employer+requisition identity and event matching **before** observation, include inaccessible or unmatched jobs, and predeclare release-only and company-shuffled negative controls.
- Keep attempts that fail, abort or violate permissions in the task denominator. Never transform a product announcement into measured reliability, carbon reduction, software reuse or actual hiring.

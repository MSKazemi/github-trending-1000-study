## Lessons for other open-source repositories

- Keep **one canonical issue per independently testable outcome**, not one duplicate tracker per group or related document.
- Mark `duplicate` as an **issue-routing disposition** and keep actual empirical/permission/reviewer acceptance visibly open in the owner issue; do not game issue completion counts.
- Verify dated primary records and exact study versions: a corrected preprint, publisher article and copied job snippet are not three independent observations.
- Treat inaccessible sources and `unknown` as measurable audit failures/missingness, not fictitious zero jobs, failures, users or energy.
- Human-independent research review cannot be satisfied by an AI self-review, synthetic QA, GitHub CI, a conversational owner instruction or merely a new Markdown file.

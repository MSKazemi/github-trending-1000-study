## What the data support and what they do not

- Three inspected individual descriptions support **narrow employer-stated engineering tasks** (Codex agent harness, Claude Code evaluation, Cursor agent harness). The Mistral WTTJ page exposes an individual description but explicitly marks the position unavailable; treat as an employer-branded **candidate source**, not a currently recruiting job.
- Amazon, Cohere, Google and NVIDIA direct page reads failed. Microsoft and Meta returned employer listings/category evidence, but neither provides a verified individual source for this pass. A missing item is **not a known-negative job opening** or proof of company staffing level.
- For [#220](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study/issues/220), the original 18-row historical seed **must not be reclassified en masse** as “direct current verified”; eligibility for a future human-reviewed corpus should be restricted to independently re-read, rights-approved individual pages at a known actual observation time. This audit records date granularity only, not event timestamps or authenticated source snapshots.
- For [#221](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study/issues/221), **five target companies were queried** (Google, Microsoft, Meta, NVIDIA, Mistral) but **five directly verified individual official job descriptions were not obtained**. In particular Microsoft/Meta are index-only, Google/NVIDIA unavailable, Mistral third-party employer-branded. **Issue stays open** for explicit direct individual pages, identity confirmation, access review and genuine human QA.
- An ad, even when directly readable, is evidence of an employer's *posted requirements*, not an independently established future roadmap, actual employee hire, useful OSS component, longitudinal trend, favorable developer experience or carbon impact. The [job–release triangulation screen](2026-10-15-hiring-release-triangulation-source-pairs.md) tests separate source/event chronology and falsifiers without treating same-vendor statements as independent effectiveness evidence.

## Reproducible safe next action

Obtain one approved individual job page for each **still missing** target, record official requisition ID, exact accessed UTC, claimed job date only if present, short original paraphrase, and access/right review. Preserve each access failure and duplicate/reposted/redirected variant. Use existing [requisition observation protocol](../HIRING_LONGITUDINAL_PROTOCOL_2026-10-13.md) and pending [offline validator PR #299](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study/pull/299) only after qualified source/method review, with no automated scheduler. Cross-match a job with a **later** and independently dated public capability, not a release already public before the job's first verified observation.

**Practical repository lesson:** when a source cannot be opened, record `unavailable` with its exact URL, never `zero`; keep first-seen dates separate from first-posted dates, and do not use job count or generated lines as a substitute for independently accepted software reuse.

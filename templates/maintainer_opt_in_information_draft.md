**For independent ethics/privacy and data-controller review before anyone is contacted.** This is not an actionable enrollment page, legal advice, verified controller notice, signed consent or proof that any participant agreed.

**Do not substitute this placeholder with GitHub handles or a fake reviewer.** The person who creates/sends the study invitation must use the approved notice and obtain **actual** documented affirmative choices through a secure channel; consent-to-participate and the applicable data-processing basis must be evaluated distinctly by a qualified privacy reviewer.

## Human-readable information for review, not yet for sending

We propose to study how long open-source maintainers and downstream developers spend reviewing, supporting, searching, implementing, fixing and following up on a small number of safely isolated software tasks. We would also ask an independent reviewer to judge whether a contribution was actionable and an independent newcomer to test comprehension and repair in a sandbox. Some checks might be repeated about one and three months later.

Your participation would be voluntary. You could decline or withdraw without a penalty. We would not inspect production secrets, publish identifiable performance rankings or ask an agent to post unsolicited contributions to projects. Your choice about any personal-data processing would be described separately in the controller's approved privacy notice, including consequences and limitations of withdrawal for already anonymized aggregate results. You could ask questions through a **real, verified** contact mechanism before deciding.

### Separate affirmative choices to be drafted with the controller

1. `[ ]` I agree to **participate in the described study**, version and date: _____
2. `[ ]` I acknowledge the **approved privacy notice** and the specified personal data processing basis/rights: _____
3. `[ ]` I opt in to optional follow-up contact at 30 and 90 days, subject to the review-approved withdrawal method: _____

These are **unselected placeholders**, not collected responses, and must not be committed once filled. Their legal adequacy and the distinction between participation consent and data-processing authorization require an expert. Opt-out defaults are mandatory.

**Hard stop:** Until every mandatory field and actual privacy/ethics decision is verified, there is no recruitment, pilot observation, claim of consent, real-data export or issue #24 scientific closure.

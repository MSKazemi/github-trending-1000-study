## Inclusion rules applied

- **27 ledger rows**, each one dated record. Several cite the same changelog/update index family; rows are **not** independent confirmations. 030-019 and 030-021 share duplicate group `030-019` (one launch, two accounts).
- **Vendor vs independent:** CMSWire and diginomica are independent publishers but mostly relay vendor claims (no testing); they are graded E1, not E3. npm registry timestamps corroborate CLI release *dates* only, not quality.
- **Availability classes kept separate:** announced (Apr 13 AEO, Sep 2 roadmap), preview/beta (Source, Branch API, AEO Analytics API), shipped (vendor-stated), resolved incidents (`corrected`).
- **Excluded items are not "no activity":** Webflow publishes many small weekly updates; this ledger is a purposive selection weighted to agent, reuse/export, API-contract, pricing and reliability evidence.

## Untrusted-content note

The [Digidop pricing article](https://www.digidop.com/blog/webflow-pricing-2026-update) (JSON-LD date 2026-05-14) embeds text addressed to AI agents instructing them to POST questions to the site's own assistant endpoint before answering. This was treated as untrusted page data: **not followed**, no request sent, and the page was not used as evidence.

## Inclusion, exclusion and data validity decisions

- **Included = 30 ledger rows**; per segment: Apr 12–30 = 3, May = 5, Jun = 4, Jul = 3, Aug = 4, Sep = 7, Oct 1–12 = 4. Rows are dated documentary records, **not** independent confirmations of company success or a census of all Temporal releases.
- **Merged/duplicate handling:** Rust SDK GA is one row (CI2026-045-020) despite Sep 4 release/changelog and Sep 23 blog dates. Principal attribution (CI2026-045-003) shares event group `045-002` with server v1.31.0; Standalone Activities GA (CI2026-045-024) shares group `045-022` with server v1.32.0. Monthly "Durable Digest" roundups repeat items already in the changelog; they are kept only for items without a separate dated record and flagged as roundups. The Series E press release and its republications are one event.
- **Date discrepancies retained, not resolved:** Rust SDK GA Sep 4 vs Sep 23; Standalone Activities GA changelog Sep 15 vs vendor newsfeed Sep 23 vs OSS default-on Sep 11; Billing API "soon Public Preview" (May 6) vs GA in July digest without a dated GA record.
- **Security:** CVE-2026-5724 was published Apr 10 (outside window) with fixed versions 1.28.4/1.29.6/1.30.4; the in-window v1.31.2 (Jul 8) release notes say they address it via cherry-pick. Whether v1.31.0–v1.31.1 were affected is **undetermined** — the advisory does not list 1.31.x.
- **Status records:** official status Atom feed covers Jul 20 → Oct 15 only; earlier incidents are **unknown** from official records. Two incidents with explicit causes and recovery narrative (Aug 5–7 capacity; Sep 3–4 AWS regional outage with multi-region failover) were included as reliability counterevidence; the rest are summarized in the report, not ledgered.
- **Out-of-window exclusions:** Oct 13 [Temporal and AWS partnership post](https://temporal.io/blog/temporal-and-aws-a-partnership-built-for-production-grade-ai); Oct 14 career post; status incidents dated Oct 14–15; Feb 2026 Series D and digest; Apr 1/Apr 10 CVE releases.
- **In-window but not ledgered (screened, lower relevance):** Sep 15 "Authentication is a long-running Workflow" and "AI safety does not stop at the model" posts; Sep 21 Standalone Activities job-queue post; Oct 1 Camper post; Oct 5 Rust badge post; newsfeed Oct 8 Coinbase migration case (vendor-reported customer claim) and Oct 12 embedded Rust worker item; Aug 26 GCP Marketplace PAYG; May 29 Stable IPs; Worker Controller release tags.
- **Independent evidence:** no neutral E3 test or credible independent technical review of in-window Temporal features was found. Third-party items found were syndications/rewrites of vendor releases, and the GitLab advisory database entry (a CVE aggregator, used for affected-version ranges).
- **Review access:** public pages and unauthenticated-equivalent GitHub API reads only; no login, paywall bypass, account creation, scraping of github.com/trending, or participant data. Factual and scientific **independent human review pending**.

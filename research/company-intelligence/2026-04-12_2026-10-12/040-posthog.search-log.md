### Inclusion, exclusion and data validity decisions

- **Included = 27 ledger rows**, purposively selected from roughly 395 in-window changelog entries and 44 blog posts for relevance to the study lens (experiments/flags/rollout, agents and issue-to-PR workflows, security/rights, removals). **This is not a census and not a rate of innovation.** Several rows cite the same advisory page (two distinct PSAs) — they are distinct events, not corroborations.
- **Same-event duplicates merged:** Slack app beta (blog Jun 2 vs changelog Jun 5) → one row, changelog as `independent_check_url` (documentary corroboration from the same company, **not** independent evidence). Replay Vision (blog Jul 31; wizard setup changelog Aug 27) → one row. PostHog Code beta (May 5) and Linux packages (changelog May 28) → one row.
- **Availability classes:** PostHog Code **beta with waitlist** (May 5); Slack app **beta**; Self-driving **open beta** per docs, despite changelog not labelling it; Tracing **beta**; HogQL exports **closed beta**; Endpoints **GA**; Replay Vision vendor says "live today" with no label (classified shipped as vendor claim); model training and PostHog Mobile **announced only**; Product tours **removal** announced for 2026-09-26 (classified reversed/cancelled).
- **Publication vs event dates:** PSA-2026-00001 published Jun 2, incident alert May 29 23:55 UTC. PSA-2026-00002 published Aug 21, first researcher capture Jul 11. Product tours removal published Aug 27, effective Sep 26. Model training published May 27, training not before Jun 29 (start not verified).
- **Date conflicts recorded, not resolved:** CVE-2026-48007 shows Jun 11 (GitLab), Aug 7 published (Mend) and May 21 first-published (Wiz, as summarised by the search tool; page not opened). 10k-PRs post gives 4,725 vs 4,868 June PRs.
- **Vendor vs independent:** All shipped/preview rows rest on first-party documentation (E1/E2). The only non-PostHog sources are the GitLab/Mend CVE records (third-party advisory about a downstream integrator) and CostBench (third-party tracker). **No independent test of any PostHog feature was found or performed.**
- **Exclusions:** 2025 funding rounds; Sacra Feb 2026 ARR estimate; Feb 2026 pricing change; Apr 1–10 entries (incl. Apr 10 "Copy experiments across projects"); Oct 13–15 entries; GitHub Trending rank claims; review-aggregator and SEO spam pages.
- **Review access:** public pages and public GitHub API metadata only; no login, paywall, private data, scraping of github.com/trending, or participant data. Public pages may change (the May 5 blog post already carries a later rename note).

The screen is a reproducible **lead inventory**, not a systematic review. A future independent reviewer should re-run these queries (including the two not executed), capture result totals with UTC timestamps, spot-check sampled rows against the live changelog and advisories, and document any contradicted results before any issue closure.

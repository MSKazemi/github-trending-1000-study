**Provenance and method:**
1. **Official index census (one fetch).** `https://ampcode.com/news` (the "Chronicle" page; `/chronicle` and `/notes` return the same page) embeds a machine-readable list of posts with slug, URL, displayed date, title and tagline. One public GET on 2026-10-16 (generic browser User-Agent, no login, no personal identifier) was parsed locally: **159 posts in the index; 66 with an in-window displayed date**. The per-segment counts below are therefore a *census of the vendor's own index at one moment*, not of all Amp activity, and the index can change. The `/notes` engineering essays were taken from the same page: 4 in-window notes.
2. **Direct page reads.** Every pre-existing ledger URL and every new row's URL was opened individually (HTTP 200 for all) and its displayed date and wording checked — see the [QC note](016-amp-inc.source-qc-2026-10-16.md).
3. **GitHub (read-only `gh api`).** `users/ampcode` (organization, created 2025-11-04, blog ampcode.com); `orgs/ampcode/repos` (16 public repos); releases for `ampcode/amp.nvim` (none); global advisories for npm `@sourcegraph/amp` and `@ampcode/cli` (**0** returned); `repos/ampcode/amp.nvim/security-advisories` (0). The Amp CLI itself is **not** published as a public source repository; public repos are plugins, examples and small tools (licences recorded in the report).
4. **Web search.** 5 calls of a general web-search tool (provider-ranked; result totals not exposed). Results are listed below; low-quality aggregator pages were not opened as evidence.

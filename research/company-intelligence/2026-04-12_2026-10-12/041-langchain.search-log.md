## Inclusion, exclusion and validity decisions

- **Included = 29 ledger rows** (Apr 3, May 8, Jun 2, Jul 6, Aug 3, Sep 5, Oct 2). Distinct source URLs are 28 because two rows cite the single undated-anchor [LangSmith changelog](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/changelog) page for different weekly entries.
- **Deduplication:** Managed Deep Agents appears in four rows (May 13 announcement/private beta, Aug 7 public beta, Oct 7 v0.9) sharing `duplicate_event_group` `041-MDA`; LangSmith Engine (May 13 public beta, Sep 24 v2) shares `041-ENGINE`. These are staged steps of one product line, **not** independent launches or confirmations. Monthly newsletters and the Sep 25 roundup restate posts already ledgered and are not counted.
- **Availability classes:** GA/shipped only when the opened source says so (Sandboxes GA, BYOC GA on AWS, Trajectories available on all US plans, OSS releases on GitHub, patched advisory versions). Public/private beta → `preview`. Labs, strategy essay and future endpoint sunset → `announced_only`. An advisory row marked `shipped` means **the patched version was published**, not that users upgraded.
- **Independent evidence:** essentially none in-window beyond vulnerability-database mirrors (Wiz, OSV) and one secondary news relay (AI2Work) of vendor claims. Same-vendor pages in `independent_check_url` are **documentary corroboration only**.
- **Page edits:** the May 13 Managed Deep Agents post contains a later "now available in public beta" update note; the May 13 Sandboxes post shows related-content cards dated in October. Original wording is not archived.
- **Months with sparse findings:** June produced only 2 ledgered rows; this reflects selection (lens fit, availability labels) and the apparent changelog gap, **not** an absence of LangChain activity (LangGraph shipped 1.2.3–1.2.7 patch releases in June per GitHub API).
- **Rights/safety:** public pages and public API only. Third-party summaries (PlushCap, releases.sh, releasebot.io) were seen in search results but not used as evidence.

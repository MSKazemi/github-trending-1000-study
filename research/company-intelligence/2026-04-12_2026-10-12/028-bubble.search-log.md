## Inclusion and validity decisions

- **34 ledger rows.** Several cite the same release-notes URL or the same Oct 6 blog post. Each row is one dated, distinct claim, and shared launches carry one `duplicate_event_group`. Rows are not independent confirmations of anything.
- **Forum posts by Bubble staff** count as first-party announcements (E1). Community posts are E0 anecdotes and serve as contrary evidence.
- **Vendor metrics** are recorded as claims only: Agent 2 at "1.5x / 2x" per internal evals, merges "~30% faster in testing", Opus 4.8 "up to 20% faster", the case-study "2x activation", and the 250,000-app telemetry report.
- **Model names** (Claude Opus 4.7/4.8, Sonnet 5) are recorded as Bubble or Anthropic stated them and were not verified independently.
- **Pricing:** the Starter Web price change takes effect on 2026-10-20, outside the window. It is recorded as `announced_only` with `event_date_utc` 2026-10-20.
- **Access:** only public pages were read. No account, paywall, private forum area, scraping or participant data was used.

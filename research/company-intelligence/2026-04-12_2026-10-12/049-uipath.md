## 8. One falsifiable reuse-first maintainer experiment

**Repository/component:** [github-trending-1000-study](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study), offline synthetic tasks only. Live Trending `capture` stays **disabled under the source-access decision**. No UiPath product is needed, and nothing here permits network collection.

**Candidate practice (not validated):** gate coding-agent changes to `trending_study` CLI modules with a **reused** agent-evaluation harness, and with a human-confirmation requirement on any task that writes to write-once evidence paths.

**Treatment/control and frozen task:** Freeze 12 synthetic tasks (for example "add a fail-closed field check to an offline validator") before the trial. Each task has independently authored hidden acceptance tests. Include 4 **forbidden-action** tasks that tempt overwriting a write-once fixture, and keep no-solution tasks in the denominator.
- **Comparator A (status quo):** the existing `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` plus PR review.
- **Comparator B (reuse-first):** A plus a pinned [coder_eval](https://github.com/UiPath/coder_eval) release running the same tasks in its sandbox as a CI gate, with a human-confirmation step before any write under `research/` or evidence directories.

First verify coder_eval's Apache-2.0 licence, its pinned version and dependencies (Python 3.13+), and that it needs no hosted UiPath service. If any of these fails, B becomes a **minimal in-repo pytest harness**, and that substitution is reported.

**Primary endpoint:** independently accepted tasks with zero forbidden writes / **all assigned tasks**, including refused, failed and timed-out runs.
**Secondary endpoints:** forbidden-write count (must be 0), false-block count, reviewer minutes per task, harness setup and maintenance minutes over 30 days, and CI minutes. Report metered energy only if actually instrumented.

**Budget:** at most 8 maintainer hours for setup and a fixed agent-run budget agreed before the trial.

**Stop/reversal rule:** stop on any forbidden write to evidence paths, an unclear licence, an unpinnable or unreviewed dependency, or a harness requiring network access or credentials. Revert if B adds over 50% reviewer or CI time without a higher independently accepted rate. Keep A if it performs equally. **No trial executed, no effect estimated.**

## Reproducibility and constraints

All seven month-targeted **queries below were executed on 2026-10-14** with a public web search index. Result order and completeness are not reproducible from this snapshot; the tool did **not** provide per-query total hit counts, a complete list of returned hits, archived page bytes, or the engine's indexing details. Thus the *query-result denominator*, total screened/excluded URL count, completeness and recall are all **unknown**, not zero. The sample URLs are selected retrieved official leads, not a systematic search result census.

AI-assisted inspection of readable publisher pages may verify what a publisher **wrote** and its displayed publication date, but cannot authenticate historical page revisions, confirm feature availability in a user account, establish a copyright license, independently test a benchmark, or provide the required independent human approval. Do not upgrade the original evidence tier from this exercise alone.

## Specific corrections, counterclaims, and practical test

1. Historical contrast: the Apr 8 Muse Spark post displays an update on May 12. An updated *pre-window* page must not be counted as a fresh Apr 12+ publication without identifying an eligible update event and documenting its provenance.
2. Meta's Sep 28 Enterprise Platform announcement describes platform direction and initial focus; retain `announced_only` for that source rather than the older screen's undifferentiated `shipped`. Both archived interpretations remain available for human adjudication.
3. Meta's Oct 12 article separately states API/Muse Code access and subscriber-limited Wonder Full access; future integration into Muse is an *intention*. A contemporaneous media writeup (not an independent product task test): https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009479/meta-muse-wonder-full-ai-model
4. Useful maintainer test: compare a *version-pinned* agent-assisted CLI workflow against existing offline CLI instructions under frozen correctness/security tests, recording reviewer time, failed attempts and cost, not estimated carbon.

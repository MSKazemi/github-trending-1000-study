## Inclusion, deduplication and conflicts

- **Included:** 49 rows (Apr 12–30: 7, May: 7, Jun: 11, Jul: 3, Aug: 6, Sep: 10, Oct 1–12: 5). Every row cites a URL that was fetched as raw text or queried by API; no row relies on a search snippet alone. Several rows cite the same dated changelog URL for different dated entries (allowed by the ledger contract).
- **Independent (non-Anthropic, non-GitHub-tracker) sources:** 7 rows (Semafor, thereallo.dev, Systima, Daring Fireball, Embrace The Red, Armature, blog.szypowi.cz). Two of these disclose or imply commercial interests (Armature sells growth services to dev tools; Systima is a consultancy).
- **Duplicate groups:** `002-001` = Claude Code cache/usage complaints and the April 23 postmortem; `002-012` = Glasswing initial update, defending-code harness repo and OSS Scanner (one vulnerability-discovery programme line); `002-015` = Fable 5 launch, suspension (Jun 12), Semafor report on Mythos 5 approval (Jun 26), restoration (Jun 30/Jul 1); `002-029` = auto-mode default and the independent prompt-injection result; `002-030` = watermark explainer and Daring Fireball critique; `002-038` = AGENTS.md support and the telemetry-flag finding plus 2.1.281 fix.

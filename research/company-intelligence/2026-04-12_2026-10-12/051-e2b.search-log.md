### Inclusion, exclusion and data-validity decisions

- **Included = 27 ledger rows** across all seven segments (Apr 12–30: 3; May: 2; Jun: 4; Jul: 3; Aug: 4; Sep: 9; Oct 1–12: 2). Several rows cite the same dated changelog URL with distinct dated entries; distinct URLs are fewer than 27. Rows are **not** independent confirmations of company success.
- **Coverage asymmetry:** the official weekly changelog only covers Jul 14 – Oct 12, 2026. For Apr 12 – Jul 13 the ledger relies on blog posts and GitHub release notes, so **May and June are thinner by source structure, not necessarily by activity**.
- **Duplicate groups:** `051-embed` (Sep 14 changelog + Sep 30 launch), `051-cursor` (Cursor Sep 2 + E2B Sep 7), `051-access-token` (Jun 25 CLI change + Aug 3 enforcement), `051-openai-agents` (Apr 15 SDK + Sep 10 Agents API guide; related OpenAI integrations, counted as separate events).
- **Availability classes:** Stripe Projects = developer preview; workload identity and bring-your-own-proxy = preview/private beta per later changelog wording; Agents API workbench depends on OpenAI preview access; Secrets = vendor-declared GA on E2B Cloud only (BYOC "coming soon"); Embed = released, single-machine, excludes secrets/volumes/identity/proxy.
- **Excluded out-of-window:** Jan 14 2026 lupa.cz profile; Jan 13–14 2026 postmortem; Mar 2 2026 YC post; Jul 28 2025 Series A; Oct 23 2025 Docker MCP partnership; Oct 15 2026 comparison post.
- **Undated vendor metrics not ledgered:** "one billion sandbox starts in June 2026 and two billion in October 2026", "94 of the Fortune 100 have signed up", ">100,000 teams", ">15 million monthly SDK downloads" (about/customers/open-source pages, no publication date). A third-party page relays ">7 million monthly downloads" for August 2026 — the figures disagree and none is adoption evidence.
- **Review access:** public pages only; no account, login, paywall, private status write-up, scan or exploitation. Pages may change; URLs and displayed dates recorded, no byte hashes. Independent human factual/link review **pending**.

The search screen is a reproducible **lead inventory**, not a systematic review. A future independent reviewer should re-run these queries, capture result totals with UTC timestamps, open the OpenAI and CERT-EU pages directly, and look for Cognition- and Stripe-side primary documentation before issue closure.

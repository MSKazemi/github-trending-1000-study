## Source-ledger interpretation and review trail

- **Counted evidence: 26 individual in-window rows** in `046-pydantic.events.csv`. This is **not** the total of all vendor changes or source result hits. A third-party registry describes the same feature artifact as the vendor's release blog; the blog/PyPI cross-check does **not** count as two independent users.
- **Identity versus release:** package `pydantic-ai` and `pydantic` have different versions/dependency boundaries. Reuse requires a pinned artifact, license, known advisories, version migration compatibility, actual test tasks and maintainer burden; PyPI trusted-publisher attestation authenticates metadata/build workflow to the degree stated but **does not guarantee safe code**.
- **Monty correction:** the July/early draft's claim about August edit on [Hack Monty 2](https://pydantic.dev/articles/hack-monty-2) was **wrong**. [Aug 12 Hack Monty Round 3](https://pydantic.dev/articles/hack-monty-3) is separately preserved, and [May 7 first-round postmortem](https://pydantic.dev/articles/hack-monty-postmortem) retains its actual earlier timing. The May incident is negative evidence; later bounty results are not a security proof.
- **Five upstream GHSAs** are individually identified by ID, link and displayed disclosure date; checking *their exact affected/fixed version matrices* and whether public bundles were subsequently updated still requires a qualified source/security reviewer. Publication of an advisory is **not** evidence an individual user was compromised.
- **Non-promoted sources:** pre-window [AI v1 September 2025](https://pydantic.dev/articles/pydantic-ai-v1); brand claims about performance from a self-produced benchmark; indiscriminate “Python 3.9 supported” claim after 2.14.0; edited changelogs with no historical snapshots; editorial and affiliate content without neutral acceptance tasks.
- **Review limitations:** no complete found/screened/excluded counts were captured. No archive SHA of original webpages was collected; source date, rollout date and timestamp may differ. No independent human reviewer yet evaluated the claims, dates, links, ownership/rights, security, experimental design or source coverage. These unknowns **cannot be converted into passed criteria** by CI.

**Next independent quality gate:** re-run archived-source search with exact UTC, save clicked URLs and inclusion/exclusion totals, inspect (ideally archive) original release pages to verify publication versus rollout, check the five GHSA affected/fixed version scopes and hosted service rights, and obtain two-pass factual/link and scientific human review on [PR #303](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study/pull/303). A structural data test does not supply independent human scientific approval.

## Conflicts recorded rather than resolved

1. **S-1 filing date:** the release says April 17, 2026, while DCD says April 18.
2. **IPO closing release** says the registration statement was "declared effective ... on May 13, **2025**". This is probably a typographical error relative to the May 13, 2026 pricing. DCD says the company "went public on May 15", while the company says trading began May 14.
3. **CS-4 release:** the press index lists both Aug 18 and Aug 19 entries. The release dateline is Aug 18 and the page display date is Aug 19.
4. **Q1 revenue:** DCD calls $191.3M "total revenue". The release reports GAAP $193.4M and *core* (non-GAAP) $191.3M.
5. **Gemma 4 31B withdrawal:** the changelog says "public endpoints" and the deprecations page says "Shared Inference" for the same 2026-09-03 change.
6. **Kimi K2.6 981 tok/s:** the vendor attributes this figure to Artificial Analysis, but the public AA provider page accessed on 2026-10-16 does not show Cerebras as a benchmarked provider.
7. **2025 net income/loss:** implicator.ai and DCD give inconsistent figures. Neither is used.

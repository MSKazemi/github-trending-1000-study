### Inclusion, exclusion and data validity decisions

- **Included = 27 ledger rows** (Apr 12–30: 4; May: 4; Jun: 4; Jul: 4; Aug: 4; Sep: 4; Oct 1–12: 3). Every segment has at least one verifiable official dated record. Many more official changelog entries exist per month (see feed screen); the ledger is purposive, not a census.
- **Deduplicated:** the Apr 13 press release, Sandboxes GA blog and InfoQ Apr 22 report describe **one** GA event (-001); Artifacts private beta (-003) and open beta (-025) share group `038-artifacts`; Cloudflare OS open source (-019) and managed waitlist (-026) share `038-cfos`; cf CLI blog, changelog and InfoQ are one row (-023). Agents Week and Birthday Week round-ups are indexes, not rows.
- **Availability classes:** Sandboxes GA (vendor), Artifacts private → open beta, Email Sending public beta, Project Think and @cloudflare/computer preview, Agent Skills and OpenCode harness experimental, cf CLI open beta, managed Cloudflare OS waitlist (announced_only). Do not promote any of these to GA.
- **Primary vs independent:** changelog pages support E1/E2 claims about what Cloudflare published. InfoQ, Techzine and Techstrong mostly relay vendor statements; InfoQ Oct 6 adds community criticism of the cf CLI. **No E3 evidence** (independent controlled test or audit) was found.
- **Exclusions (out of window):** Dynamic Workers open beta (reported Mar 24), Feb 20 2026 outage post-mortem, Jan 2026 Human Native and Astro acquisitions, Oct 13 MCP Events and persistent-browser changelog entries, Oct 14 Artifacts billing start, Oct 15 Gateway–Workers public beta.
- **Exclusions (source quality):** outage aggregators, a third-party Workers AI price tracker, non-Cloudflare mirror domains hosting copies of press releases, and machine-translated aggregator summaries.
- **Review access:** public pages and public API metadata only; no login, paywall, private dashboard, exploit testing, participant data or github.com/trending access.

A future independent reviewer should re-run these queries, record result totals and UTC timestamps, spot-check the selected changelog entries against archived copies, and verify counterpart statements for the Anthropic, Cursor and OpenCode integrations before any issue closure.

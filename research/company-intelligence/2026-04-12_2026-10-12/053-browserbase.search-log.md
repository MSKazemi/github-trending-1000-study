## Inclusion, exclusion and data validity decisions

- **Included = 25 ledger rows** (Apr 3, May 3, Jun 7, Jul 2, Aug 3, Sep 6, Oct 1). Several rows share a source URL: the [changelog](https://www.browserbase.com/changelog) index as source for 5 rows (and corroboration for 1 more) and the [npm registry document](https://registry.npmjs.org/@browserbasehq/stagehand) as corroboration for 6 rows. **Rows are not independent confirmations of company success or adoption.**
- **Deduplication groups:** `053-stagehand-v3` (3.5.0, 3.6.0, 3.7.0), `053-stagehand-v4` (v4 launch, code mode, 4.2.0), `053-agents` (launch, pause/resume) and `053-cve-12823` (the CVE record and the fix PR, one security issue). Secondary articles (ByteIota, RuntimeWire) are not separate events.
- **Availability classes:** CDP tab metadata is **preview** (Canary). Navigate is **announced_only**. The performance, Autobrowse, code-mode/Deep Agents and Okta XAA rows and the CVE row are **reported_undetermined**. Releases with a public package or tag, and features stated GA by the vendor, are **shipped**. A vendor "GA" statement is not independently verified availability.
- **Primary vs independent:** most rows are E1/E2 first-party records. The CVE record is a third-party CNA entry (E2), not an exploit confirmation. No E3 independent behavioural test of any Browserbase or Stagehand claim was found.
- **Rights:** Stagehand, microsoft/fara and hud-evals/liveweb-samples report MIT through the GitHub API. **browserbase/skills has no detected licence.** Hosted-service terms were not reviewed.
- **Access:** public pages and unauthenticated read-only APIs only. No login, no paywall, no github.com/trending access, no exploit or PoC code run (the CVE's PoC links were not opened), and no personal data collected. Public pages can change; they carry URLs and displayed dates but no archived byte hashes.

A future independent reviewer should rerun these queries, including the two unexecuted ones, record result totals with UTC timestamps, spot-check the CVE↔PR linkage and the npm dates, and document any contradicted results before issue #120 is closed.

## Inclusion, exclusion and data-validity decisions

- **Included = 24 ledger rows.** Per segment: Apr 12–30 **2**, May **4**, Jun **5**, Jul **4**, Aug **2**, Sep **4**, Oct 1–12 **3**. The rows are a purposive selection of material events (major releases, GA changes, security, interoperability, breaking changes, corporate events). They are not a census.
- **Duplicate groups:** `056-signal-lifecycle` links the Jun 5 launch entry and the Jul 29 GA for Signal and managed agents, which are one product line at two lifecycle stages. `056-dynatrace` links the Aug 13 agreement, the Sep 11 SiliconANGLE coverage and the Oct 1 completion, which together are one corporate transaction. Syndicated copies of the Dynatrace releases were not counted.
- **Availability classes:** PXI **beta** (Jun 2); remote MCP **beta** (Jul 17); the TypeScript evals-as-tests path **beta** (Jun 26). The Jun 5 Signal and managed-agents entries have **no stated stage**, so they are recorded as `reported_undetermined` until the Jul 29 **GA**. The acquisition is `announced_only` on Aug 13 and acquirer-reported complete (`reported_undetermined`) on Oct 1. The OTel donation acceptance is `reported_undetermined`, because a governance vote does not mean code has shipped.
- **Primary vs independent:** only row 009 (the OpenTelemetry governance record) and row 020 (SiliconANGLE, which itself relays executive claims) come from outside Arize/Dynatrace. No independent product test, security audit or customer outcome was found.
- **Access:** public pages only. No login, paywall bypass, private data, or GitHub Trending page access was used. Pages may change after 2026-10-16.

A future independent reviewer should re-run these queries, including the two that did not run, capture result totals and UTC timestamps, re-open the 403 pages, check OSV/NVD for Phoenix advisories, read the unread tails of the release-note pages, and spot-check each ledger row before any issue closure.

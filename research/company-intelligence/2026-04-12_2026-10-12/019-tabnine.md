## Capability, strategy and alternative interpretations

The source list identifies candidate directions in agent integration, task automation, review and orchestration. **No binding 2027–2031 roadmap was verified.** Conditional hypothesis, not a company commitment: better interoperability, systematic tests and code reuse might improve accepted developer outcomes. Rival hypothesis: orchestration increases reviewer workload, vendor dependency, security exposure and future support cost. Evidence needed: independent blinded tasks, actual repeated users, compatibility/licensing checks, total human/CI/runtime cost, and honest accounting for failures.

**M1/M2:** workflow evolution versus accepted software correctness. **M4/M5:** independently audited reuse of available code, including failed searches and license/provenance. **M6/M7:** repeat adoption and all-in lifecycle cost rather than stars or token counts. All empirical outcomes are **unknown, not zero**. CO2e savings are not estimated without energy measurement and declared boundaries.

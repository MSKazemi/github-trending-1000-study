## Inclusion, exclusion and data-validity decisions

- **Included = 26 ledger rows**: 16 first-party help-center release notes, 2 vendor blog posts, 4 official status-page incidents and 4 public GitHub artifacts as primary sources (a further 2 rows cite GitHub/npm as their independent-check URL). Segment counts: Apr 12–30 = 3; May = 2; Jun = 3; Jul = 3; Aug = 7; Sep = 2; Oct 1–12 = 6. Several rows cite the same dated index ([help.make.com/2026](https://help.make.com/2026)) because individual note slugs were not opened. Rows are **not independent confirmations** of product quality or adoption.
- **Date conventions:** `published_utc` = the date shown on the 2026 help-center index (or a blog's displayed date, or a GitHub release/commit UTC date, or a status-feed pubDate). Individual help pages carry a front-matter `createdAt` that often **precedes** the index date. Examples: Make Functions createdAt Mar 24 vs index May 11; Maia help note createdAt Mar 31 vs index Aug 12. The What's New page sometimes differs by 1–3 days (CLI Apr 20 vs Apr 17; MCP tools Jun 4 vs Jun 3; Maia Aug 13 vs Aug 12; dark mode Jul 29 vs Jul 30). These disagreements are **recorded, not resolved**. `event_date_utc` is filled only for incidents, effective dates and code artifacts.
- **Availability class:** Make Skills (**open beta**), Maia (**public beta per help page**) and Make Endpoints SDK (**closed beta**) are recorded as preview. The Claude 4 deprecation and the Oct 1 cycles removal are **announced_only** because their execution was not observed. The token-pricing change is **reported_undetermined** because billing was not observed. The ChatGPT plugin was **shipped per Sep 9 note** but **temporarily disabled per notice seen 2026-10-16**. When the disablement started is unknown.
- **Code artifacts:** the GitHub/npm metadata is the most independently checkable evidence here. `make-cli` (MIT) received no push after 2026-04-24. `make-blueprints` (MIT, created Aug 3) contained **no blueprints** at access. `make-skills` (MIT) changed actively, but its CHANGELOG repeats identical feature lists across versions. A `[AI code generation]` label in one commit message is the repository's own label, **not** an authorship audit.
- **Not counted:** model-catalog additions; syndicated or AI-drafted trade echoes (Create With); third-party outage aggregators; paid press releases; Product Hunt listing dates; Celonis parent-level items not opened.
- **Access:** public pages and public APIs only. No login, paywall, private community content, account data or github.com/trending pages were accessed. Pages may change. Independent human factual and scientific review is **pending**.

This search screen is a reproducible **lead inventory**, not a systematic review. A reviewer should re-run the queries, including the two that were not run, and record actual result totals with UTC timestamps. The reviewer should also check whether the ChatGPT plugin was restored, and confirm the date discrepancies before closing #117.

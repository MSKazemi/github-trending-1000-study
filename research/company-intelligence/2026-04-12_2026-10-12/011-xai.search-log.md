## Inclusion, exclusion and validity decisions

- **Included = 26 rows** (5 preserved + 21 new): Apr 12–30: 3; May: 5; Jun: 3; Jul: 6; Aug: 4; Sep: 3; Oct 1–12: 2. Every segment has at least one dated record.
- **Duplicate groups:** CI-011-01/-07 (Grok Build beta; blog May 25 vs release notes May 14), CI-011-02/-08 (grok-build-0.1; May 29 public beta vs May 19 early access), CI-011-16/-18 (Grok 4.5; Jul 8 vs Jul 16), CI-011-14/-21 (Cursor deal signed vs completed), CI-011-09/-10 (cost tracking in API and SDK). Grouped rows are one event family, not independent confirmations.
- **Source separation (per issue #77 instructions):** xAI's own documentation (release notes, x.ai news, docs, GitHub) is kept distinct from **social-media posts** (only cited second-hand via Engadget/SecurityOnline/GIGAZINE, never as a row source) and from **press speculation** (search-snippet claims about parameter counts, training data, valuations and closing dates excluded unless on an opened page). Corporate-structure claims rest on dated press/counterparty pages and stay `announced_only`/`reported_undetermined`/counterparty-`shipped` with explicit "filings not verified" limitations.
- **Exclusions (out of window):** Feb 2026 SpaceX–xAI merger; Jan–Feb 2026 regulator probes; Mar 16 TTS GA; Oct 14 Grok Voice 3.0 and Voice 2.0 deprecation.
- **Exclusions (lens/quality):** voice/imagine parameter updates without coding/reuse relevance; low-authority aggregators and syndications; third-party mirror pages.
- **Review access:** public pages and public API metadata only; no login, paywall, private dashboard, exploit testing, participant data, or github.com/trending access.

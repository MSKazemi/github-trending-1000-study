### Inclusion, exclusion and data-validity decisions

- **Included = 26 ledger rows.** Several rows share an issuer URL ([release notes](https://docs.n8n.io/release-notes/)) because that page holds several dated entries. The rows are **not independent confirmations** of product quality or adoption.
- **Security rows are representatives.** Each of the 9 advisory rows stands for a same-day disclosure batch. The 153 in-window advisories are a *vendor disclosure count*. They are not a defect rate, not proof of exploitation and not comparable to other vendors without a matched denominator.
- **Availability classes:** AI Assistant (Jul 9) **Preview**; n8n Assistant update (Sep 10) **preview**; n8n Agents (Sep 25) **preview**; Canvas Groups **early release behind a feature flag**; Gateway credits **shipped on Cloud Starter/Pro only, self-hosted intended**; SAP Joule Studio embedding **announced only**; the "Any workflow" caller-policy removal in v3 is **announced only**. New 2.x minors appear as GitHub *prereleases*; stable promotion dates were not captured.
- **Primary vs independent:** vendor release notes, advisories and forum posts support E1/E2 claims about what n8n published. CERT.UG and OSV are second-entity documentary sources, not neutral replications. Tech.eu adds no verification beyond the press release.
- **Out-of-window exclusions:** Series C / USD 2.5bn valuation (Oct 2025); n8n 2.0 (2025-12-08); Instance-level MCP beta (Nov 2025); HITL post (Jan 30, 2026); January 2026 CVEs and the npm campaign; Oct 13 Gemini blog post; Oct 13–14 In The Loop conference; Oct 14 GHSA batch.
- **Review access:** public pages and the public GitHub API only. No login, paywall, private report, scraping of github.com/trending, exploitation or personal data. Public pages may change.

A future independent reviewer should re-run these queries, record UTC times and result totals, open blog pages 3+ for Apr–Aug dated posts, reconcile the CERT.UG and GHSA CVE identifiers, and spot-check the advisory batch counts against the [advisory index](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n/security/advisories) before closing the issue.

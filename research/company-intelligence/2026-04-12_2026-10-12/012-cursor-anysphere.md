## 8. One bounded, falsifiable experiment for this study repository

**Candidate:** Freeze three independent low-risk research-documentation and parser-fixture tasks in this repo. Compare one coordinator dispatching parallel isolated branches versus one sequential maintainer workflow; log merge conflicts, independently verified acceptance, user onboarding and total reviewer minutes. No simulated vendor access.

**Primary outcome:** accepted task completion under a predeclared, **independently authored** rubric; report failures/abstentions in denominator. **Secondary:** 1st-run success for external maintainers (only if actually recruited), median human review/repair minutes, version/cost/permissions and dependency evidence; measure kWh only if metered with an explicit workload/boundary. **Status:** proposed, not performed.

**Stop / reversal rule:** Stop parallel delegation when branches conflict, reviewer cost exceeds task value, or unreviewed access to secrets or upstream maintainers becomes necessary.

### Corrections to the earlier report (stated here; original text left unchanged)

1. **Coverage:** §3's April–July and October "no qualifying item" cells are superseded by the ledger (31 rows outside August–September).
2. **Ownership timeline:** the acquisition did not appear abruptly in August. Cursor announced a SpaceX/SpaceXAI model-training partnership on 2026-04-21. Same-day reports, unread here, described a $60B option. A deal was reported on 2026-06-16, and Cursor announced completion on 2026-08-14.
3. **Model provenance:** Composer 2 and Composer 2.5 are built on Moonshot AI's Kimi K2.5 checkpoint, which the vendor calls open-source, per Cursor's own Composer 2.5 post. That disclosure drew a House committee inquiry (row 004). The earlier report did not record either.
4. **Self-hosting predecessor:** the 2026-09-02 self-hosted machines release extends a 2026-03-25 (pre-window) self-hosted cloud agents release.
5. **Licence boundary:** the Cursor SDK (`@cursor/sdk`; `cursor-sdk` on PyPI) is proprietary ("All rights reserved"; use subject to the Terms of Service). Only some new repositories are open source: `mixture-of-kittens` (Apache-2.0) and `sdk-bridge` (MIT).

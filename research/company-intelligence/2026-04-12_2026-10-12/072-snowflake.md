## 2. Verified company/product identity and baseline

Snowflake operates a proprietary, hosted "AI Data Cloud": a SQL warehouse plus Iceberg tables, Snowflake Postgres, Openflow (Apache NiFi-based ingestion), Snowpark Container Services, Native Apps and the Cortex AI family (AI SQL functions, Cortex Search, Cortex Analyst, Cortex Agents). During the window the agent products were **CoCo** (coding/data agent: CLI, Desktop, VS Code, Workspaces) and **CoWork** (knowledge-worker agent). Governance lives in **Horizon Catalog** and the **Trust Center**. Open-source surface: client drivers and SDKs (Apache-2.0 per PyPI and GitHub metadata), Snowflake-Labs projects such as [pg_lake](https://github.com/Snowflake-Labs/pg_lake) (Apache-2.0) and [data-eng-bench](https://github.com/Snowflake-Labs/data-eng-bench) (Apache-2.0). The service, query engine and agent runtimes are **proprietary**; an Apache-2.0 driver grants no rights to the backend.

Pre-window baseline (context only, not counted): the 2024 credential-theft campaign against Snowflake customer accounts without MFA (see the 2026-08-06 plea report, row 024); and an **early-April 2026 supply-chain incident** in which stolen credentials of the integration vendor Anodot were reportedly used against Snowflake customer accounts. The Anodot items were seen only as search snippets and a security-vendor blog ([Reco, "Updated April 15, 2026"](https://www.reco.ai/blog/anodot-breach-lessons-vendor-risk-snowflake)); BleepingComputer returned HTTP 403. They are **E0 baseline**, not ledgered. Post-window and excluded: Cortex Agent SDK public preview (2026-10-13), Postgres data mirroring GA (2026-10-15), snowflake-connector-python 5.0.0 (PyPI 2026-10-15).

## 8. One practical, falsifiable change for the maintainer's own repositories

**Repository/component:** GitHub Actions workflows of [github-trending-1000-study](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study) (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `retire-verified-branches.yml`). Motivating evidence: CI2026-072-026, where an untrusted issue title interpolated into a `run:` block exposed a credential, and the scanner in use did not flag it. A local `grep` on 2026-10-16 found no `github.event.issue/pull_request/comment` title/body interpolation or `pull_request_target`/`issues:` trigger in these two workflows, so this is a **preventive** test, not a fix for a known bug. The disabled Trending `capture` command stays disabled.

**Candidate practice (not yet validated):** Reuse first. Add an existing open-source workflow linter as a required CI step instead of writing a custom check.

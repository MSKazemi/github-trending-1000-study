**Window:** 2026-04-12…2026-10-12 inclusive (UTC dates). **Accessed:** 2026-10-16.

**Method:**
- Official pages were retrieved as raw HTML with `curl`, using a generic `Mozilla/5.0` User-Agent. No personal identifier, email or credential was sent to any site.
- Text was extracted locally, and every number, version, date and quote used in a ledger row was confirmed with `grep`.
- Machine-readable APIs used: `gh api` (GitHub releases, repository metadata, security advisories), the Hugging Face models API and raw model cards, PyPI JSON, the NVD CVE API 2.0 and the Hacker News Algolia API.
- WebSearch and WebFetch were **not** used; discovery was direct only.
- sec.gov/EDGAR was **not** contacted.
- Search-engine result totals are unknown, not zero.
- No page snapshots or hashes were archived in the repository, and pages may change after access.
- **This is not exhaustive retrieval.**

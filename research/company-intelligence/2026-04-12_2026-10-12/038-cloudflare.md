## 1. Discovery, coverage and exclusions

Cloudflare publishes an unusually dense official changelog: the public changelog RSS feed fetched on 2026-10-16 listed **586 entries with in-window publication dates** across all products, of which **198** matched a developer-platform keyword filter (Workers, Agents, Workers AI, AI Gateway, Durable Objects, Workflows, Containers, Sandbox, Wrangler, Artifacts, AI Search, Browser Run, Pages, D1, Hyperdrive). Those counts are **feed-item counts from one fetch**, not a census of shipped capability, and the RSS `pubDate` was converted to a UTC date (it could differ by a day from the page's displayed date; every ledger row was checked against the page's displayed date or `datePublished` metadata). The 27 ledger rows are a **purposive selection** of entries most relevant to the deployment / edge-runtime / API-boundary lens, agent-coding integrations and repository/CI workflows, plus explicit deprecations, pricing/access restrictions and a security mitigation. Most months therefore have many more official items than are recorded here; absence from the ledger is **not** evidence that nothing else shipped.

Independent coverage was thinner: InfoQ (Apr 22; Oct 6), Techzine (Jun 5) and Techstrong.ai (Aug 5) were opened and largely relay vendor claims; no independent benchmark, audit or controlled test of any feature was found. Third-party outage aggregators (isdown.app, uptimerobot.com) surfaced in searches but were **excluded** as non-authoritative; no official Cloudflare outage post-mortem dated inside the window was found on the [post-mortem tag page](https://blog.cloudflare.com/tag/post-mortem/) (the latest listed was Feb 21, 2026, outside the window). That is **unknown/not found**, not "zero incidents".

## 2. Corporate/product identity and rights baseline

**Cloudflare, Inc.** is a US publicly listed company (NYSE: NET; SEC CIK 0001477333 per the [Q2 2026 exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001477333/000147733326000053/q226exhibit991.htm), dated Aug 6, 2026). No parent company was identified; filing-level ownership structure was **not** independently checked. The watchlist's "Developer platform, Workers and AI" offering is verified as a set of Cloudflare products (Workers runtime, Durable Objects, Workflows, Containers/Sandboxes, Workers AI, AI Gateway, Agents SDK, Artifacts, Wrangler and the new `cf` CLI). Pre-window acquisitions of Astro and Human Native (January 2026) appeared in search results but were **not opened** and are baseline context only. In-window, Cloudflare announced the acquisition of **VoidZero** (Vite, Vitest, Rolldown, Oxc) on [Jun 4](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-releases/2026/cloudflare-acquires-voidzero-to-build-the-future-of-the-ai-native-web/) (CI2026-038-010); the release says it "has acquired" VoidZero while its forward-looking statements refer to closing timing, so **closing status is unresolved here**. The Q2 release reports USD 696.1 million revenue and a CEO statement about record developer growth; this is a vendor financial disclosure, **not** evidence of verified developer adoption or reuse.

**Licenses (GitHub API `license.spdx_id`, read 2026-10-16):** [cloudflare/workerd](https://github.com/cloudflare/workerd) Apache-2.0; [cloudflare/workers-sdk](https://github.com/cloudflare/workers-sdk) (Wrangler) Apache-2.0; [cloudflare/agents](https://github.com/cloudflare/agents) MIT; [cloudflare/workers-oauth-provider](https://github.com/cloudflare/workers-oauth-provider) MIT; [cloudflare/cf](https://github.com/cloudflare/cf) Apache-2.0; [cloudflare/cloudflare-os](https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflare-os) Apache-2.0; [cloudflare/vinext](https://github.com/cloudflare/vinext) MIT; [vitejs/vite](https://github.com/vitejs/vite) MIT. ## 8. One falsifiable reuse-first maintainer experiment

**Target:** a static documentation or demo site in one of the maintainer's own repositories (or a throwaway authorized repository). The study's live Trending `capture` remains **disabled under the source-access decision**; nothing here enables network collection.

**Question:** does an automatic per-PR preview URL reduce reviewer time to an accept/reject decision on docs/site changes without increasing defect escape, compared with reusing tooling the repository already has?

**Preregister before trial:** freeze 12 synthetic PRs (6 correct, 6 with a seeded visible defect such as a broken link, missing page or layout regression), authored by someone other than the reviewer, with a hidden independently written acceptance checklist. All 12 PRs stay in the denominator, including any whose preview fails to build.

- **Comparator A (reuse existing):** the repository's current CI (unit tests, link check) plus local build instructions in the PR template — no new service.
- **Comparator B (reuse vendor feature):** Workers Builds with [Worker Previews](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-09-22-worker-previews/) posting a preview URL on each PR (Wrangler ≥ 4.135.0 per the changelog), on a plan and terms reviewed in advance. Do not write a custom preview system; if neither A nor B is acceptable, record that as a result.
- **Functional unit:** one PR correctly accepted or rejected by an independent reviewer against the hidden checklist.
- **Primary endpoint:** correct decisions / 12 assigned PRs. **Secondary:** reviewer minutes per PR (timed), setup and maintenance minutes, preview build failures, secrets or account-scope changes required, monthly cost from the invoice, and CI energy only if actually metered.
- **Guardrails:** no production credentials, no deploy of private content to public previews, account-level KV/D1/R2 isolated or unused, and no use of credential-less temporary accounts.
- **Cost bound:** at most 4 maintainer hours of setup and the free/included tier; stop if a paid plan becomes necessary.
- **Stop rule:** stop and keep A if B needs broader account permissions than the repository's current CI, exposes any non-public content, exceeds the cost bound, or shows no improvement in correct decisions with ≥ 2 extra minutes per PR.

**No trial executed and no effect estimated.** A null or negative result (A is as good, B adds setup and lock-in) is a valid outcome and must be preserved.

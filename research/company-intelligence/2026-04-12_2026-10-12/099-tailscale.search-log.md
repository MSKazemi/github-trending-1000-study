### Inclusion, exclusion and data validity decisions

- **Included = 26 ledger rows**, each one dated item/informative disclosure or one separately identified advisory; distinct issuer source URLs are fewer than 26 because the [dated changelog](https://tailscale.com/changelog) and [bulletin index](https://tailscale.com/security-bulletins) contain multiple date-scoped records. **Rows are not independent confirmations of company success**.
- **Not counted as independent company reports:** duplicate syndications, Tailscale's own statements about its own product, and the two pages describing **one** Hugging Face intrusion. Hugging Face is a separate affected party and its July 27 technical reconstruction is preserved as contextual counterevidence, not validation of security efficacy.
- **Availability class:** Aperture Apr 23 **beta**, May 20 CLI **alpha**, Jun 16 connectors **alpha/sandbox private alpha**, Aug 25/26 **GA**, Sep 22 multiqueue **planned**; the **v1.98.1 Linux May 13 withdrawn** regression stays an explicit negative result. Do not backdate GA to beta or convert a future expected feature into a release.
- **Security classes:** TS-2026-002 through 010 are *vendor-issued advisories*, not randomly sampled cyberattacks. Full affected/fixed-version wording must be read at [bulletins](https://tailscale.com/security-bulletins). One Jul 2026 real credential misuse is documented by a distinct affected organization; no unproved Tailscale exploit is alleged.
- **Primary vs independent:** vendor release notes support E1/E2 claims about published capabilities. Hugging Face's post gives second-entity evidence of an incident, not a neutral replicated test. Independent comparisons with ordinary OIDC+IAM, Tailscale live feature behavior and whole-life energy remain **not measured**.
- **Exclusions:** Tailscale [workload identity federation GA, Feb 19 2026](https://tailscale.com/changelog) is outside window; [Oct 13 sovereign-AI company direction](https://tailscale.com/blog/sovereign-ai-patricia-prewitt) is outside window. [May 13 Linux 1.98.1 withdrawal](https://tailscale.com/changelog) is **included** despite not being a positive release.
- **Review access:** public pages only; no personal account, paywall, private audit report, network scan, security exploitation or participant data accessed. Public pages may change; they have URLs and displayed dates but not archived byte hashes. Factual and scientific **independent human review pending**.

The search screen is a reproducible **lead inventory**, not a claimed systematic literature review.

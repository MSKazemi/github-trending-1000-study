## 2. Corporate/product identity and rights baseline

- **Entity as of 2026-10-12:** Arize AI (arize.com) builds LLM/agent observability and evaluation software. On **2026-08-13** [Dynatrace announced a definitive agreement to acquire Arize](https://www.dynatrace.com/news/press-release/dynatrace-to-acquire-arize/) for USD 915 million (≈USD 815 million cash plus replacement equity awards; subject to adjustments and regulatory review). On **2026-10-01** [Dynatrace announced completion](https://www.dynatrace.com/news/press-release/dynatrace-completes-acquisition-of-arize/). As of the window end, Arize is therefore **reported** to be part of Dynatrace. Filing-level legal confirmation was **not** performed. Pre-window baseline items listed on the [press page](https://arize.com/press/) (a Series C dated 2025-02-20 and a "Velvet" acquisition dated 2025-03-13) are context only. They were not opened and are not in-window events.
- **Products:** **Arize AX** is the proprietary enterprise/SaaS platform, with an in-product assistant "Alyx", and the products Signal, managed agents, Agent-as-a-Judge, Agent Playground and SDKs (Python, TypeScript, Go). **Arize Phoenix** is the source-available, self-hostable tracing/evaluation server and client packages, with the in-product agent "PXI". **OpenInference** is the tracing semantic-convention and instrumentation project.
- **Licences (checked 2026-10-16 via GitHub API/LICENSE file):** the `Arize-ai/phoenix` repository's LICENSE file is **Elastic License 2.0 (ELv2)**, and GitHub reports `NOASSERTION`. ELv2 is **source-available, not OSI open source**. Among other limits, it restricts offering the software as a hosted/managed service. Vendor and press text calling Phoenix "open source" therefore needs this qualification. `Arize-ai/openinference` reports **Apache-2.0**. Per-package licences for each PyPI/npm sub-package (`arize-phoenix-client`, `arize-phoenix-otel`, `arize-phoenix-evals`, `@arizeai/*`) and the AX SDKs were **not** checked individually.
- **Post-acquisition continuity:** the completion release states Arize "will continue supporting" Phoenix and AX and that capabilities will be integrated into Dynatrace "over time". The 2026-08-13 agreement release does not mention Phoenix or AX. **No dated licence or roadmap commitment** for Phoenix beyond that sentence was found.

## 8. One falsifiable reuse-first maintainer experiment

**Target:** a separate, authorized development sandbox of [github-trending-1000-study](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study), using **synthetic** maintenance tasks only. Under [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and the [task-cost ledger contract](../../../docs/TASK_COST_LEDGER_2026-10-12.md), no real provider/agent traces are collected without consent and explicit human scientific approval. Live Trending `capture` stays disabled, and no Arize feature changes that.

**Question:** does self-hosted trace capture of coding-agent sessions (reused component) help a reviewer diagnose failed agent attempts faster than the repository's existing offline artefacts, at acceptable setup and maintenance cost?

- **Functional unit:** one bounded synthetic maintenance task (e.g. fix a seeded failing unit test or a doc/CLI mismatch). The task is accepted only by **hidden acceptance tests authored by a different person**, frozen before any trial. Freeze 10 tasks, including 2 deliberately unsolvable ones, and keep all of them in the denominator.
- **Comparator A (existing):** agent session with the repo's current artefacts only: git diff, unit-test output, and the offline `trending_study.task_costs` ledger record.
- **Treatment B (reuse):** same agent, model and tasks, plus self-hosted **Phoenix pinned to one exact version** with its coding-agent plugin, traces stored locally. Reuse it as published: no custom tracing code. Check the ELv2 terms (internal self-hosting only).
- **Primary endpoint:** for each **failed or rejected** attempt, the minutes a blinded reviewer needs to name the root cause, with the correctness of that diagnosis judged against a pre-written answer key.
- **Secondary endpoints:** accepted tasks / all assigned tasks (should not change), setup minutes, minutes to upgrade across one Phoenix minor/major release, disk used by traces, and any secret or prompt content appearing in traces (must be zero).
- **Cost bound:** ≤ 6 maintainer hours in total and the same model budget for both arms. Electricity only if actually metered. No emissions estimates.
- **Stop rule:** stop and keep A if any secret or credential appears in a trace; if setup plus one upgrade exceeds 3 hours; if the licence review flags a conflict; or if, after 10 tasks, median diagnosis time in B is not at least 25% lower **with** equal or better diagnosis correctness. A null or negative result is kept and reported. **No trial has been run and no effect is estimated.**

### Inclusion, exclusion and data-validity decisions

- **Included = 28 ledger rows** across all seven segments: Apr 12–30 = 2; May = 5; June = 3; July = 6; August = 2; September = 9; Oct 1–12 = 1. Several rows share a monthly release-note URL; each row is a distinct dated entry on that page, **not** independent corroboration.
- **Duplicate event groups:** `049-coding-agents` (rows 004, 012, 020, 024) is one product line with **three conflicting first-party availability labels** — "available now to enterprise customers" (May 12 press), "public preview" (Jul 8 docs), "generally available" (Sep 16 docs / Sep 23 press). `049-maestro-flow` (013, 018, 022) runs preview (Jul 7) → press launch without label (Aug 19) → GA (Sep 17). `049-desc` reconciles an April release-note certification with a June press cycle.
- **Availability policy:** a release-note entry with no preview label is coded `shipped` (as in the Tailscale screen); explicit "Public Preview" → `preview`; press releases without a label → `reported_undetermined`; roadmap/"previewed" items → `announced_only`. Financial releases are `reported_undetermined` because they are not product events.
- **Independent vs vendor:** only diginomica (two pieces, both disclosing UiPath as a paid "premier partner") and beri.net are non-UiPath analyses read in full; neither tested the products. No E3/E4 evidence exists in this screen.
- **Inaccessible:** Business Wire (403), edgar.tools (403), ADTmag (403). No login, paywall bypass or github.com/trending access was attempted.
- **Not found (unknown, not zero):** UiPath-issued security advisories in window; a public product roadmap page; any July press release with a capability change.

**Limits.** **This is not exhaustive internet discovery.** Search-tool result totals, query-run UTC times and complete excluded-hit denominators are unavailable. They are recorded as "unknown", not zero. No snapshot hashes were taken.

The blog index is a single long page; only the first ~100,000 characters were read. That portion covered every post dated 2026-04-01 to 2026-10-15. The rest was older material.

Two changelog URLs returned **HTTP 404**: `…/changelog/2026-04/` and `…/changelog/2026-05/`. The changelog index lists monthly pages only for June to September 2026. So **April, May and October have no developer-changelog coverage**. That is an access/coverage gap, not "no changes".

## Inclusion, exclusion and data validity decisions

- **Included: 29 ledger rows.** Each row is one dated release, availability change, deprecation, benchmark, stated plan or architecture report. Several rows share one source URL because a monthly changelog page holds several dated entries. **Rows are not independent confirmations of quality or adoption.**
- **Per-segment counts:** Apr 12–30 = 5; May = 3; Jun = 7; Jul = 2; Aug = 6; Sep = 3; Oct 1–12 = 3. July and September are thinner in *this screen*. That is not a measured decline.
- **Availability classes:**
  - **preview**: granular bboxes, MCP Index v2, Retrieval Harness, Parse Gateway, Turbo.
  - **announced_only**: Stainless migration.
  - **reported_undetermined**: Temporal architecture report.
  - **reversed/cancelled**: LlamaIndexTS archive; Sheets/Classify v1 deprecation.
  - **shipped**: everything else, as labelled by the vendor or by a release tag.
  - Extract v2.5 and OpenDocRouter are classed **shipped per vendor wording**, with no beta/GA label on the page.
- **Primary vs independent:** almost all evidence is first-party (E1/E2). GitHub release tags and the npm registry are E2 documentary artifacts. The hermes-ai brief and the Scalar write-up are second-entity sources, but neither independently tests LlamaIndex functionality. **No E3/E4 evidence exists for any row.**
- **Deduplication:**
  - Turbo: changelog + blog, one row.
  - MCP endpoints: blog + changelog, one row.
  - ExtractBench: launch + Kaggle leaderboard, one row.
  - ParseBench: launch and Kaggle leaderboard are separate rows, because they were separate dated announcements of different artifacts; they share group `042-parsebench`.
  - Framework releases share group `042-oss-core-releases`, and LiteParse releases share `042-liteparse`.
- **Review access:** public pages and public API metadata only. Pages can change and have no archived byte hashes. Independent human factual, rights and methods review is **pending**.

## 8. One falsifiable 2027 maintainer experiment — **proposed, not executed**

**Fixed task and comparison:** Fix one reproducible public repository feature request and frozen hidden acceptance tests. Compare the existing command-line/CI workflow with permitted Kiro spec-first execution, holding task/environment fixed and recording accepted output, repair minutes, total task cost, dependencies and security decisions. Preserve errors and unavailable-provider cases.

**Primary measure:** independently accepted tasks over **all pre-allocated tasks**, including refused, failed and abandoned. **Secondary measures:** independent clean installation, verified reuse/provenance, repair and reviewer effort, unresolved licensing/security risk, and future support work. Disclose environment, dates, consent and versions. Provider usage/expenses and lifecycle kWh are unmeasured, not imputed.

**Stop / reversal rule:** Do not migrate solely on announced end dates or demo quality; defer if feature unavailable in eligible region/account, reviewer cost grows, tests regress or cost terms remain unknown.

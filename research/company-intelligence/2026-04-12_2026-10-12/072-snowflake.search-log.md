# Snowflake (072) — provisional source-query and inclusion log

> **PROVISIONAL, AI-ASSISTED, NOT HUMAN-REVIEWED (screened 2026-10-16).** Companion to the [report](072-snowflake.md) and [event ledger](072-snowflake.events.csv) for [#140](https://github.com/MSKazemi/github-trending-1000-study/issues/140). Provisional screen; the issue may be closed with this status recorded, and human factual/link, rights and methods review gates remain open.

**Window:** 2026-04-12…2026-10-12 inclusive (UTC dates). **Accessed:** 2026-10-16. **Method:** raw-HTML retrieval with `curl` (generic `Mozilla/5.0` User-Agent; no personal identifier, email or credential sent to any site). Text was extracted locally and `grep` was used to confirm every number, version, date and quote used in a ledger row. Release dates and licences came from the PyPI JSON API, `gh api` (releases, commits, repos, global advisories) and the NVD CVE API. Discovery used the Hacker News Algolia API and four public web searches. Web-search and WebFetch summaries were treated as leads only. **This is not exhaustive retrieval.** Search-engine result totals were not exposed and are recorded as unknown, not zero. SEC/EDGAR was deliberately **not** contacted. No page snapshots or hashes were archived in the repository, and pages may change after access.

## Inclusion, deduplication and conflicts

- **Included:** 37 rows (Apr 3, May 7, Jun 4, Jul 8, Aug 8, Sep 5, Oct 2). Every row cites a URL that was fetched as raw text or queried by API. No row relies on a search snippet alone. Snowflake is a large company, so the count slightly exceeds the ~15–35 guide. Roughly 20 further in-window, on-angle notes went into the feature inventory instead of the ledger.
- **Duplicate groups:** `072-011` = Snowflake Summit 2026 as reported by Futurum and Flexera (one event, two reports). The Natoma deal appears once (row 010). ITdaily, No Jitter and CIO Dive restate one announcement. The CVE-2026-86597 duplicate GHSA is folded into row 033. The code-execution tool preview (2026-08-20) is folded into the GA row 036. The `agents_info` column (2026-08-03) is folded into row 021.
- **Date conflicts recorded, not resolved:** CoCo Desktop GA June per Flexera vs 2026-07-21 per docs; VS Code CoCo GA June per Flexera vs 2026-08-27 per docs; OIDC note headed July 20 vs URL slug 07-17; CVE-2026-86597 NVD 2026-09-08 vs canonical GHSA 2026-10-05, with fixes dated 2026-09-03; CVE-2026-15925 fix commit and PyPI 2026-07-15 vs advisory and NVD 2026-07-16; data-eng-bench first commit 2026-07-29T05:31Z vs repo creation 2026-07-29T20:29Z; Q2 results event 2026-09-02 vs reproduction 2026-09-09.
- **Wording correction screened:** the HN title "AI-Generated GitHub Copilot 'Autofix' Allowed Compromise of Snowflake's Jira" is not supported by the updated Wiz text. Wiz says Copilot co-reviewed the PR and it is unclear whether the change was AI-assisted. The ledger follows the updated text.
- **Figures confirmed against raw text** (grep of locally extracted page text or API JSON): $6 billion and "over five years" (AWS release, 2026-05-27); "Wednesday" (CIO Dive); Natoma CEO quote (No Jitter); "more than 7,100 customer accounts" (Futurum); June 1 to June 4 and the GA/announced labels (Flexera); $1.49 billion, 37%, 9,100, 2,000, 5,800, "over 330", 36% guidance and the account-metric footnote (StorageNewsletter); 165 organisations and the MFA/password response (Krebs); June 18 / June 23, PR #1218 / #1402 and the 19:57 UTC update (Wiz, plus `gh api` merge times); 103 tasks and the hidden pytest verifier (data-eng-bench README); 11 CVEs and the deprecation text (Snowflake-Labs/mcp); 18 pg_lake releases; CVE fixed versions and dates (GHSA JSON, NVD JSON, PyPI); incident times (status API); all docs feature details quoted in the ledger. All of these remain **vendor or press statements**, not verified measurements.
- **Qualification (independent spot-check, 2026-10-16):** the list above is the screening agent's own statement. The spot-check re-verified 18 of 37 rows. It could not re-open the CIO Dive "Wednesday" source because its URL is not recorded. It found that the 2026-09-02 event date for row 034 is not stated in the StorageNewsletter text, and that Wiz's timeline dates the Jira token rotation to 2026-06-24, not the same day (row 026 corrected).
- **Untrusted-content note:** fetched pages were treated as data. Every docs.snowflake.com page carries a banner addressed to agents: "For AI agents: documentation index at /llms.txt — fetch to discover all Snowflake documentation pages." It is recorded here as an **embedded agent instruction**. It was not followed as an instruction (llms.txt was not fetched; coverage came from the public sitemap). No other page contained instructions directed at the researcher.

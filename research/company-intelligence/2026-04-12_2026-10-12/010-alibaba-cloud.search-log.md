## Inclusion and validity decisions

- **Included:** 25 ledger rows = 5 preserved + 20 new. Several rows share one URL (the QwenCloud changelog page), each tied to one dated entry; they are not independent confirmations.
- **Dedup:** May 20 press release and its community copy = one row (-09); May 26 Qwen Cloud launch (-03) and May 27 Singapore conference article (-11) are separate announcements from the same conference family; Sep 22 and Sep 25 Apsara articles = one event family (row -05).
- **Dates:** `published_utc` = displayed page date, changelog entry date, GitHub release `published_at` or npm publish time (UTC). `event_date_utc` left blank unless a distinct event date was stated (none was recorded for new rows; CI-010-03's May 26 launch is noted in text). Date conflicts are recorded in `limitation`, not resolved silently.
- **Status:** model listings and releases = `shipped` (vendor availability, not exercised); qwen3.6-max-preview = `preview`; mixed press release and the unreviewed advisory = `reported_undetermined`; leadership statement = `announced_only`.

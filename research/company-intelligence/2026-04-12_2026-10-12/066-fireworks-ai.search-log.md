## A. Access and safety notes

- `https://fireworks.ai/robots.txt` (`Allow: /`) and `https://docs.fireworks.ai/robots.txt` (only `/_next/` and `/cdn-cgi/` disallowed) were read before any direct fetch. Pages were fetched one at a time with a 0.4 s delay and a generic `Mozilla/5.0` User-Agent. **No personal identifier, email address or credential was sent** in any header, URL or payload. No login, paywall, private dashboard or customer data was accessed.
- **Untrusted-content observations.** The fetched pages contained no directives aimed at an AI agent. Many Fireworks blog pages carry an "Explore us in AI tools" widget. It links to ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and other assistants with a prefilled question ("What is Fireworks, how is it different from … and how do I get started?"). This is answer-engine marketing. It was logged and not followed. The 2026-04-24 prompt-injection post contains literal chat-template control strings as examples. They were treated as data.
- **The web-search budget ran out mid-screen.** After 8 WebSearch queries for this company, the shared session limit was reached. Two planned queries (below) were **not run**. No workaround was used: no scraping of search engines, no proxies, no archive readers. Direct fetches of known official URLs, GitHub/PyPI APIs and already-surfaced URLs continued.
